Health and lifestyle survey analysis

Behavioural health data on a national scale, read for the habits that shape long-term risk.

  • Average BMI landed at 28.54 — the overweight band — alongside an average alcohol-consumption reading of 5.19

  • Depression rose steeply among participants with poor general health and unhealthy habits, and was more common among women
  • BMI category breakdown with filters by sex, so each finding can be checked per group

What moves student results
